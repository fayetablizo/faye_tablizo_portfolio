Automatic Rain-Sensing Clothesline Using Arduino

An Arduino clothesline that pulls itself in when it rains. A rain sensor is installed on the roof or outside the house to detect water. When the weather is dry, the sensor sends a HIGH signal to the Arduino and the clothesline stays outside. Once the sensor detects water or rain, it sends a LOW signal. The Arduino then commands the motor to pull the clothesline back under the roof or shelter, so the clothes do not get wet.

  • Arduino
  • Rain sensor
  • Motor
